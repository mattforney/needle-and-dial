How it works

Every station runs on a fixed weekly schedule that your browser computes from the same starting moment, so anyone tuned to a station hears the same program at the same second. Dramas start on the hour or half hour; station IDs, vintage commercials and musical interludes fill the gaps, the way a real station hit its marks.

Nothing is hosted here. Every second of audio streams straight from the Internet Archive's servers, which is why the dial goes quiet if archive.org is down. If you use this, donate to the Archive.

Rights, station by station

    Needle & Dial is a prototype, not affiliated with the Internet Archive. Station call letters are fictional. Audio and metadata: Internet Archive contributors, Old Time Radio Researchers, LibriVox, the Great 78 Project, the Live Music Archive tapers, Musopen and the netlabels credited on air.
